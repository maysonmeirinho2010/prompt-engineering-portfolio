[TAG Prompt Template]

> **Task:** I'm stuck solving quadratic equations by completing the square.  
> **Action:** Walk through one example step-by-step and then give me a similar practice problem.  
> **Goal:** Understand the method well enough to do it on my own.

## Overview

This prompt helps you get step-by-step help with hard math topics. It solves the problem of getting stuck on homework when you don't have a teacher nearby. It is useful for middle school students who need help studying math.

**Best for:**
- Getting step-by-step help with hard homework
- Studying for math tests
- Practicing topic problems on your own

**Structure:** T-A-G (Task, Action, Goal)

**Technique:** Zero-shot

**Output:** One clear example with steps, plus one practice problem.

---

## Quick Start

1. Open [`prompt.md`](./prompt.md) and copy the template.
2. Replace the placeholders:
   - `[PLACEHOLDER_1]`: Your math topic (like quadratic equations)
   - `[PLACEHOLDER_2]`: What kind of practice problem you want
3. Paste it into your AI model of choice and run it.
4. Review the output and adapt it to what you need.

## Customization Tips

- **Want more detail?** just ask the AI to break down the math steps even more.
- **Want it shorter?** Ask for just one practice problem without the extra example.
- **Different context?** Change the math subject to science, history or English.
