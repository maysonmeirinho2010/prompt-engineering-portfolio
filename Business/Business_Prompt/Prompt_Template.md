# Late Package Apology Template

## Overview

**Purpose:** This prompt writes a polite email to say sorry to a customer when their package is late.

**Structure:** C-A-R-E (Context, Action, Result, Example)

**Technique:** Few-shot

---

## The Prompt

**[CONTEXT]:**
Our business, [COMPANY_NAME], shipped an order late to our customer, [CUSTOMER_NAME]. 

**[ACTION]:**
Write an apology email explaining that the package is delayed. Offer them a 15% discount on their next order.

**[RESULT]:**
The customer should feel heard, respected, and happy with the discount.

**[EXAMPLE]:**
Here is an example of the tone I want:
"Dear Sam, we are so sorry your shirt is late. We know waiting is hard. Here is a 10% off code for next time to make it up to you!"

---

## Context and Inputs

- **[COMPANY_NAME]:** The name of your store. The AI needs this to sign the email.
- **[CUSTOMER_NAME]:** The buyer's name. This makes the email personal.

---

## Output Requirements

**Format:** A short email with a subject line.

**Constraints:** Do not make up fake reasons for the delay. Keep it under 100 words.

**Tone and Style:** Friendly, professional, and very polite. Reading level should be easy.$0
